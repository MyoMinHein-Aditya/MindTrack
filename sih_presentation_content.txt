Project: MindTrack
======================================================

SLIDE 1: Title Slide
------------------------------------------------------
• Project Title: MindTrack - From Silent Struggle to Timely Support
• Problem Statement: Development of a Digital Mental Health and Psychological Support System for Students in Higher Education
• Team Name: [Your Team Name]
• Submitting Members: [Your Names]
• Tagline: A privacy-first platform bridging the gap between student distress and human-led support.

SLIDE 2: The Problem
------------------------------------------------------
• The Issue: Students face immense academic, financial, and social pressure, leading to anxiety, burnout, and emotional distress.
• The Gap: Students rarely seek help early. Current support models are entirely reactive (waiting for the student to walk into a counselor's office).
• The Flaw in Existing Tech: One-time surveys miss longitudinal trends. Generic AI chatbots hallucinate or fail to handle crisis situations safely.

SLIDE 3: Our Proposed Solution (MindTrack)
------------------------------------------------------
• What it is: An early-intervention digital wellbeing system designed exclusively for higher-education institutions.
• How it works: 
  1. Detects changing trajectories through weekly check-ins.
  2. Provides immediate, safe AI-assisted first-line support.
  3. Seamlessly escalates high-risk cases to qualified human counselors.
• Core Philosophy: AI does not make clinical decisions. It serves as a supportive bridge to human professionals.

SLIDE 4: Key Features (Student Experience)
------------------------------------------------------
• Longitudinal Check-ins: Weekly tracking of mood, stress, and sleep visualized via an interactive trend dashboard.
• Gamification: Streak tracking (🔥) and Points (⭐) to incentivize continuous wellbeing monitoring and build healthy habits.
• Resource Hub: 24/7 access to curated, evidence-based coping strategies and national Indian mental health helplines (e.g., Tele-MANAS).

SLIDE 5: Key Features (Safety & AI Chat)
------------------------------------------------------
• MindBridge Chat: A conversational AI companion integrated globally across the application.
• Two-Pass Safety Layer: 
  - Pass 1: A deterministic safety classification layer evaluates every message in real-time.
  - Pass 2: If a crisis (self-harm, severe distress) is detected, the AI executes an emergency protocol, provides helpline numbers, and immediately triggers a backend risk alert.

SLIDE 6: Key Features (Counselor Operations)
------------------------------------------------------
• Role-Based Access Control: Secure, segregated dashboards for Students, Counselors, and Admins.
• Triage & Priority Queue: Counselors receive real-time, explainable 'Risk Events' in their dashboard when a student triggers the AI safety layer.
• Action-Oriented: Counselors can instantly see the highest-priority students, intervene, and mark alerts as resolved, closing the support loop.

SLIDE 7: Technical Architecture & Tech Stack
------------------------------------------------------
• Frontend: React.js, Vite, React Router, Recharts (for data visualization), Lucide Icons, Framer Motion (animations).
• Backend: FastAPI (Python), SQLAlchemy (ORM).
• Database: SQLite (can be easily scaled to PostgreSQL).
• AI Integration: Groq API (Llama 3 8B) for ultra-low latency, highly safe conversational generation.
• Security: JWT-based authentication, strict RBAC (Role-Based Access Control), privacy-first data handling.

SLIDE 8: Impact & Feasibility
------------------------------------------------------
• Scalability: Can be deployed across any university or college with isolated, secure workspaces for different schools.
• Cost-Effective: Built using open-source tools and highly efficient APIs.
• Real-world Impact: Reduces counselor burnout by automatically triaging cases, while ensuring no student falls through the cracks during a mental health crisis.
